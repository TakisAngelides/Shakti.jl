Antarctica

The Antarctic Ice Sheet from Yelmo restarts at 32 km and 16 km, the same restarts as the FastHydrology.jl examples. The default sheet, one year of 1-day steps. The setup is the one of Greenland: load_yelmo in docs/make_figures.jl, RegularizedCoulombSlidingLaw(0.25) with Yelmo's basal speed, no surface input. Grounded cells below flotation that connect to the ocean are set to OCEAN, so the grounding line is where Yelmo's ice would float.

Under most of the ice sheet the water pressure is close to the overburden. Near the grounding line the water drains into the ocean at the ocean's hydrostatic pressure, below the overburden of the grounded ice, so N/p_o rises there. The ice streams carry turbulent flow (Re up to about 10^4), the interior is laminar.
