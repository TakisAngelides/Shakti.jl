Thwaites

Thwaites Glacier on the 2 km grid of (Kazmierczak et al., 2024), with their inverted friction coefficient. The default sheet, one year of 1-day steps. The loader is load_thwaites in docs/make_figures.jl, which also makes the figure.

  • The data's x and y are swapped so the sea is on the left.
  • Mask: grounded ice from the data, cells with H = 0 excluded, grounded cells below flotation that connect to the ocean set to OCEAN.
  • A per second, smoothed over three passes (the data has jumps between hardness classes).
  • Sliding: the regularized Coulomb law of Kazmierczak et al. with their C and v0 (RegularizedCoulombV0SlidingLaw), speeds from the SSA velocity.
  • No surface input; the water comes from basal melt.
using Shakti

d = load_thwaites()                       # (; xc, yc, DX, mask, H, zb, A, G, ub_x, ub_y, C, v0)
Nx, Ny = size(d.H)
grid = Grid(Nx, Ny, (Nx - 1) * d.DX, (Ny - 1) * d.DX)
sl = RegularizedCoulombV0SlidingLaw(grid, d.C, d.v0)
b = fill(1e-3, Nx, Ny)

p = ModelParameters()                     # defaults
state = State(grid)
set_initial_conditions!(state, grid, p, sl, d.mask, d.A, d.zb, d.zb .+ d.H .+ b, b, d.G, d.ub_x, d.ub_y,
                        zeros(Nx, Ny), zeros(Nx + 1, Ny), zeros(Nx, Ny + 1))

ps = PicardSolver(500, 1e-6, CUDSSDirectSolver(grid; structure = :general), grid)   # LUDirectSolver(grid) on CPU
sim = Simulation(grid, state, 365, floattype(86400.0), p, "fully_implicit", ["N", "b", "Re", "po"],
                 ConstantMeltInput(), sl; ps = ps, which_observer = "IO", which_file_writer = "NetCDF",
                 tracked_times = [0, 365], path = "thwaites.nc")
run!(sim)

Wall times are on the Performance page.

Over most of the bed the water pressure is close to the overburden (N/p_o near 0), as in Kazmierczak et al. The water drains through a branching network that converges on the main trunk, where the flow is turbulent (Re up to 10^4). In the isolated patches at N/p_o = 1 the gap is open but holds no water, so p_w = 0: unfilled cavities allow this, filled cavities would put a negative p_w there.
